# Limitations Draft

## Answer-key era concentration

The corrected 30-case answer-key subset is era-skewed: 13/30 query cases are from the 1980s, compared with five from the 1970s, nine from the 1990s, two from the 2000s, and one from the 2010s. Authority-recovery findings should therefore be read as results for this source-verified subset rather than as an era-balanced characterization of Indian legal research.

## Residual recovery limitations

Temporal pre-ranking improved the final retrieval outcome, but it does not explain all remaining misses. The inventory records that `2013_35` remained absent despite 79 eligible candidates in its former raw top-100, whereas `1980_105` was recovered despite only three eligible raw candidates and became a top-5 hit after the final filter. This contrast indicates that eligible-pool size and lexical relevance or authority-ranking quality are independent contributing factors. Perfect verification of displayed citations therefore should not be interpreted as complete expected-authority recovery.

## Temporal granularity

ILDC query dates are available only at year granularity. The enforced policy is consequently strict earlier decision year rather than a full day-level temporal ordering rule. The final evaluation reports only this implemented temporal applicability rule; claims about finer chronology would exceed the available query-date precision.

## Explanation-quality evidence is not independent

The Week 13 review is a seven-case, non-random author self-review fallback. Its 7/7 structured preference and descriptive ratings cannot be reported as independent human-review evidence, statistically generalizable usability evidence, or evidence of legal correctness. The finding is a documented design observation, not a definitive user-study result.

## Boilerplate uncertainty language

For `2013_35`, the review found that both formats used identical generic uncertainty boilerplate despite high evidence coherence. The language was visually clear but substantively uncalibrated: it signalled the same caution level as cases with apparent retrieval noise. This is a system-level limitation rather than a format-specific defect.
